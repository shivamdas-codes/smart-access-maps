PRODUCT MANAGEMENT CASE STUDY

About the concept

Wayfinder is an independent prototype exploring how a mapping product could make accessibility trade-offs clearer.

01 / PROBLEM

Route choices hide important trade-offs

Travelers may need to balance travel time, transfers, walking distance and accessibility confidence. A single “best route” can miss what matters to an individual.

02 / HYPOTHESIS

Make preferences visible before departure

If people can state what matters and compare route attributes side by side, they may choose journeys that better fit their needs and feel more prepared.

03 / MVP

Preference-led route comparison

Route cards, step-free preferences, route detail, issue reporting and a saved-journey flow form the prototype's core experience.

04 / VALIDATION

Research before scaling

Test with wheelchair users, people with temporary mobility constraints, caregivers and everyday commuters. Validate data quality and avoid implying unverified access.

Product principles

  • Never label a route accessible when critical attributes are unknown.
  • Show uncertainty and last-verified information clearly.
  • Let users control trade-offs rather than imposing one definition of “best”.
  • Design for screen readers, keyboard navigation and high-contrast readability.
